You are solely responsible for ensuring that all information provided in your listing — including title, description, pricing, photos, and location — is truthful, accurate, and not misleading. RentNest reserves the right to remove any listing that violates these standards without prior notice.

  • Photos must represent the actual property, service, or item being listed.

  • Duplicate or spam listings are strictly prohibited and may result in account suspension.

3. Marketplace Transaction Policy

Sections 3–5 apply specifically to items sold through the Marketplace category. Rental, roommate, service, and convention hall listings are not subject to escrow holds or the commission fee described below.

For Marketplace transactions, RentNest facilitates trust between buyers and sellers through an admin-mediated escrow process. No funds are transferred directly between parties. Instead:

🔒 Escrow Hold: Payment from a buyer is sent to and held by the RentNest admin team, not the seller. Funds are only released to the seller after the buyer confirms receipt of the item.

Please note: at this stage, payment collection and disbursement are handled manually by the RentNest admin team (e.g. via mobile financial services), not through an automated payment processor. Processing times may vary and are not instantaneous.

4. Platform Service Fee (15%)

💳 Commission: RentNest charges a 15% platform service fee on every successfully completed Marketplace transaction. This fee is deducted from the payment before the remaining 85% is disbursed to the seller.

Example: If a buyer pays BDT 10,000 for a listed item, RentNest retains BDT 1,500 (15%) and the seller receives BDT 8,500 (85%). This fee covers escrow handling, dispute mediation, and platform maintenance.

5. Failed or Disputed Transactions

A Marketplace transaction may be considered failed or disputed if:

  • The seller declines to proceed after payment has already been received by RentNest.
  • The item is not delivered within a reasonable agreed timeframe.
  • The buyer claims the item is materially different from, or damaged compared to, what was listed.
  • Either party raises a formal dispute with RentNest.
🔄 Resolution Policy:
  • If the seller declines the sale after payment is received, the full amount is refunded to the buyer, and no fee is charged.
  • If a dispute is raised after delivery (e.g. item not as described, or not received), RentNest will first ask both parties to resolve the matter directly. If unresolved, RentNest will review available evidence (such as messages, photos, or timestamps) and determine a fair outcome at its sole discretion — which may include a full refund to the buyer, full release to the seller, or a partial division of the held funds between both parties, depending on the circumstances.
  • Refunds, where applicable, are processed within 5–7 business days of a resolution being reached.
  • Users found to have repeatedly acted in bad faith (e.g. non-delivery, false claims, ghosting) may face listing suspension or account termination at RentNest's discretion.
